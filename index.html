Why self-study, and why small airports

The same MLIT report found that small and medium airports can usually hire only a few foreign staff at a time, so they cannot assign a dedicated trainer the way the large airports (Haneda, Narita, Kansai) can. Because ground-handling procedures differ by airline and airport, the report proposes reducing the Japanese-language part of the training load instead: online material that learners can study alone, and teaching material in やさしい日本語. RampTalk JP is built for that suggestion.

Source: same report, p. 20.

Where the starter words come from

The 24 starter words, readings and Japanese meanings are taken from the ground-handling glossary in MLIT's employer manual for Specified Skilled Workers (pp. 19, 22). The manual notes that the many technical terms slowed on-the-job learning, that procedures differ by airline and airport, and recommends visual materials and self-study. English meanings were added for this prototype.

Source: MLIT, "Manual for accepting and retaining Specified Skilled Workers in aviation (airport ground handling)", for employers. https://www.mlit.go.jp/koku/content/001846771.pdf
